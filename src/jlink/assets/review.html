How decisions are applied

Accept pins a link, even below the threshold or without a model judgment. Reject removes a candidate. Unsure releases a previous constraint. Probabilities and original links stay unchanged. Conflicting accepts stop application. Remaining candidates use the declared cardinality, threshold and margin, with margins recomputed among feasible candidates.

After export, run: jev-link review apply review.json -o reviewed-links.json. Use --csv reviewed-links.csv for a spreadsheet-safe display copy.

The page shows original selection, plus pending manual constraints. It does not claim to show the recomputed assignment. Local storage is a convenience; the exported JSON is the durable record. Independent amendments to the same history branch must be reconciled explicitly.
